🚀Industry 4.0 & 5.0

From steam to smart — and what comes next.

Quick Definition

Industry 4.0 = connected smart factories (IoT + AI + cyber-physical systems). Industry 5.0 adds humans & sustainability back to the centre.

Industry 4.0 vs Industry 5.0

🏭 Industry 4.0

  • Focus: Efficiency & automation
  • Driver: Data & connectivity
  • Role of humans: Reduced
  • KPI: Cost ↓, OEE ↑

🌱 Industry 5.0

  • Focus: Humans + planet
  • Driver: Personalisation & ESG
  • Role of humans: Empowered by tech
  • KPI: Wellbeing, sustainability, resilience
Key Facts
  • ✓ Term coined at Hannover Messe 2011 (Germany)
  • ✓ Backbone tech: OPC-UA, 5G, edge computing, digital twins
  • ✓ Industry 5.0 launched by the EU Commission (2021)
  • ✓ Predicted > $3.7 trillion economic impact by 2030
  • ✓ Pairs with TPM + OEE to actually deliver results
Real Industrial Example
🏭

Siemens Amberg plant — one of the most digitised factories on earth. ~75% of value chain is automated, every product gets a unique digital twin, defects down to 12 DPMO. Operators use AR glasses to guide setups.

Quick Check

Q: A factory has PLCs, robots, and SCADA. Is it Industry 4.0?

Tap to reveal answer ↓ A: No — that's Industry 3.0 (automation). Industry 4.0 requires the data to leave the local machine — connecting machines, cloud, suppliers and customers so AI can act on it. Without IoT + analytics + cyber-physical loops, you're still in 3.0.
