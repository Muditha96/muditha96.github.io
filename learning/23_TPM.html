🛠️TPM

Total Productive Maintenance — the way world-class plants hit 85% OEE.

Quick Definition

TPM is a culture and method where operators and maintenance team up to keep every machine running like new — zero breakdowns, zero defects, zero accidents.

👷 Autonomous Maintenance

Operators take ownership — daily cleaning, lubrication, tightening, and inspection of their machines. They spot small issues before they become breakdowns.

Example: Operator wipes oil leak → catches loose bolt → prevents bearing failure.
The 3 Maintenance Strategies
🔥
Reactive

Wait for it to break. Cheapest per repair, costliest overall.

📅
Preventive (TPM)

Service on schedule. Reduces breakdowns by 70%+.

📊
Predictive

Sensors + AI predict failure before it happens.

Key Facts
  • ✓ Born in Japan at Nippondenso (1971) — Toyota supply chain
  • ✓ 8 pillars total — 4 core shown here

Real Industrial Example
🏭

A tire factory rolls out TPM: operators clean & inspect press machines daily, log abnormalities. In 18 months, unplanned breakdowns drop 78% and OEE climbs from 58% to 81%.

Quick Check

Q: A factory only repairs machines after they fail. Why is this expensive even though "repair only when needed" sounds efficient?

Tap to reveal answer ↓ A: Reactive failures are unplanned → production stops, scrap, missed deliveries, secondary damage (a bad bearing wrecks the shaft). The "saved" PM hours are dwarfed by lost output. TPM trades a little planned downtime for a lot of avoided unplanned downtime.
