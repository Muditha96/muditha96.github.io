🎯Servo Motor

Closed-loop precision — the heart of motion control.

Quick Definition

A servo motor is a closed-loop motor system that continuously compares actual position to commanded position using an encoder, correcting any error in real time.

Stepper vs Servo

Stepper Motor

  • ↻ Open loop
  • ⚠️ Can skip steps
  • 🔋 High torque at low speed
  • 💰 Cheaper

Servo Motor

  • 🔁 Closed loop
  • 🎯 Always corrects
  • ⚡ High dynamic response
  • 💎 Premium accuracy
Key Facts
  • ✓ Encoder feedback → arc-second positioning accuracy
  • ✓ Very high acceleration & fast settling time
  • ✓ AC servos: 3-phase brushless + permanent magnets
  • ✓ Commanded via Profinet / EtherCAT / pulse-direction
  • ✓ Top brands: Yaskawa, Mitsubishi, Beckhoff, Siemens, Delta
Real Industrial Example
🤖

A pick-and-place robot uses 4–6 servo axes to grab a component, position it within 20 µm, and place it on a PCB — completing the cycle in under 200 ms.

Quick Check

Q: A stepper "thinks" it moved 100 steps. Why might a servo know the truth and the stepper not?

Tap to reveal answer ↓ A: Servos have an encoder constantly measuring actual position and the drive corrects errors. A stepper runs open-loop — if load is too high, it skips steps silently and never knows.
