📊Six Sigma DMAIC

Data-driven defect elimination — 3.4 defects per million.

Quick Definition

Six Sigma is a statistical method to reduce process variation. DMAIC is its 5-phase roadmap: Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control.

DPMO = Defects Per Million Opportunities. Most plants run at 3–4σ. World-class & aerospace: 6σ.
Key Facts
  • ✓ Born at Motorola (1986); scaled by GE under Jack Welch
  • ✓ Belts: Yellow → Green → Black → Master Black Belt
  • ✓ Heavy use of statistics: SPC, ANOVA, regression, DOE
  • ✓ DMADV variant for designing new processes (Design)
  • ✓ Pairs with Lean → "Lean Six Sigma"
Real Industrial Example
🔧

A fastener supplier runs DMAIC: thread reject rate cut from 4.2% → 0.18%. Saves $1.2M/yr in scrap + warranty claims. Sigma level jumps from 3.2σ to 4.7σ.

Quick Check

Q: How is DMAIC different from a simple 5-Why analysis?

Tap to reveal answer ↓ A: 5 Whys is fast & qualitative — opinions chained together. DMAIC is statistical & rigorous — every step demands data, hypothesis tests, and a Control plan to lock the gain in place. Use 5 Whys for daily issues; DMAIC for chronic, $$$$ problems.
