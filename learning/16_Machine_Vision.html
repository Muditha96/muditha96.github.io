📷Machine Vision

Cameras + AI — the inspection workforce that never blinks.

Quick Definition

Machine Vision uses industrial cameras + image processing to inspect, measure, identify, or guide robots — replacing human eyes on the production line.

Key Facts
  • ✓ Components: camera + lens + lighting + processor
  • ✓ Lighting is often more important than the camera itself
  • ✓ Modern systems use deep learning for cosmetic defects
  • ✓ Interfaces: GigE Vision, USB3 Vision, Profinet
  • ✓ Top brands: Cognex, Keyence, Basler, Omron, Zebra
Real Industrial Example
💊

A pharmaceutical packaging line uses a Cognex camera to verify each blister pack has 10 pills, the lot code is correct, and the seal is intact — at 600 packs per minute.

Quick Check

Q: A vision system can't reliably detect a defect. What's usually the first thing to fix?

Tap to reveal answer ↓ A: Lighting. 80% of vision failures are lighting problems — wrong angle, glare, or low contrast. Better lighting beats a bigger camera or smarter algorithm almost every time.
