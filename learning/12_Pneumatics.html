💨Pneumatics

Clean, fast, simple — power by compressed air.

Quick Definition

Pneumatics uses compressed air (typically 6 bar) to move pistons and rotary actuators — driving most clamping, pushing, and gripping motions on the factory floor.

Key Facts
  • ✓ Typical line pressure: 6 bar (87 psi)
  • ✓ Force = Pressure × Piston Area (F = P × A)
  • ✓ Clean, safe, fast — no fire / electric shock risk
  • ✓ Compressible → springy, not for precise position control
  • ✓ Top brands: Festo, SMC, Parker, Camozzi
Real Industrial Example
📦

A case-packer uses 12 pneumatic cylinders — to push, lift, clamp, and seal cardboard boxes — cycling 60 times per minute with no maintenance for months.

Quick Check

Q: A cylinder has an 80 mm bore and runs at 6 bar. What's its extend force?

Tap to reveal answer ↓ A: Area = π × (0.04)² = 0.00503 m². Force = 6 × 10⁵ Pa × 0.00503 m² ≈ 3015 N (≈ 307 kg). Big force from cheap compressed air.
