⚡VFD

Variable Frequency Drive — control any AC motor's speed.

Quick Definition

A VFD is a power electronics device that changes the frequency of AC supplied to a motor — letting you smoothly vary speed, save energy, and protect the motor.

Why VFDs Matter

Energy Savings on Pumps & Fans

up to 50%

Halving speed cuts power by ~87.5% (cube law). Pumps and fans running at part-load save massive energy with a VFD vs. throttling.

Key Facts
  • ✓ Speed ∝ Frequency (RPM = 120 × f ÷ poles)
  • ✓ Smooth start = no inrush current, less wear
  • ✓ Built-in overload, short-circuit & thermal protection
  • ✓ Modes: V/Hz, Sensorless Vector, Closed-Loop Vector
  • ✓ Top brands: ABB, Siemens, Danfoss, Yaskawa, Schneider
Real Industrial Example
💧

A water pumping station uses a VFD to match pump speed to demand. Old setup: full-speed pump + throttle valve. New setup: VFD slows the motor — cuts energy use by ~40%.

Quick Check

Q: Why does halving a fan's speed cut its power by ~87.5%?

Tap to reveal answer ↓ A: The affinity laws: power varies with the cube of speed. Half the speed → (½)³ = 1/8 the power. That's why VFDs deliver huge savings on centrifugal pumps & fans.
