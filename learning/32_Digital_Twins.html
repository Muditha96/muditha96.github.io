👯Digital Twins

A live virtual copy of the real machine — Industry 4.0's killer app.

Quick Definition

A Digital Twin is a real-time virtual replica of a physical asset (machine, line, factory) — fed by live sensor data and used to simulate, predict, and optimise.

Key Facts
  • ✓ Inputs: IoT sensors, IO-Link, OPC-UA, CAD/PLM data
  • ✓ Platforms: Siemens NX, Azure Digital Twins, AWS IoT TwinMaker
  • ✓ Use cases: predictive maintenance, virtual commissioning, training
  • ✓ Saves ~30% on commissioning time + 25% on energy
  • ✓ Core enabler of Industry 4.0 & Smart Factories
Real Industrial Example
✈️

Rolls-Royce runs a digital twin for every jet engine in service. Live data from 1000+ sensors feeds the twin in flight — predicting bearing wear, optimising fuel, scheduling maintenance before failure.

Quick Check

Q: How is a Digital Twin different from a regular CAD model or simulation?

Tap to reveal answer ↓ A: A CAD model is static — built once, never updated. A simulation runs offline with assumed inputs. A Digital Twin is live — bidirectionally linked to its physical asset via real-time data, evolving alongside it for its entire lifetime.
