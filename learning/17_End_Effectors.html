🦾End-Effectors

The "hand" of every robot — without it, the arm is just an arm.

Quick Definition

An end-effector (or gripper) is the tool at the end of a robot wrist — chosen for the part: parallel jaws, vacuum cups, magnets, or soft fingers.

Parallel Gripper

Two jaws close on the object via servo or pneumatic actuation. Strong grip, predictable force, great for rigid parts.

Best for: Metal blocks, gears, machined parts
Key Facts
  • ✓ Gripper choice = ~50% of automation success
  • ✓ Quick-change tool plates allow multi-task robots
  • ✓ Pneumatic, electric, or hydraulic actuation
  • ✓ Force feedback enables gentle grasping
  • ✓ Top brands: SCHUNK, OnRobot, SMC, Festo, Soft Robotics
Real Industrial Example
🍅

A fruit packing line uses soft silicone grippers to gently pick tomatoes without bruising — handling 80 fruits per minute where parallel jaws would crush them.

Quick Check

Q: A robot must pick a flat steel plate from a pallet. Which gripper makes the most sense?

Tap to reveal answer ↓ A: A magnetic gripper. It needs no clearance around the part, holds anywhere on the surface, and releases instantly by cutting current — perfect for flat ferrous sheets.
