🌐Profinet

Real-time Ethernet for modern factory automation.

Quick Definition

Profinet is an industrial Ethernet protocol that delivers high-speed, deterministic communication between PLCs, drives, and I/O over standard Ethernet cables.

Key Facts
  • ✓ Runs on standard Ethernet (100 Mbps / 1 Gbps)
  • ✓ Real-time cycle times down to 31.25 µs (IRT mode)
  • ✓ Star topology via industrial switches
  • ✓ Supports motion control & safety (PROFIsafe)
  • ✓ Backed by Siemens — dominant in Europe
Modbus vs Profinet

Modbus RTU

  • ⏱️ ~50 ms cycle
  • 🔌 RS-485 serial
  • 🐢 Slow polling
  • 💰 Cheap & simple

Profinet

  • ⚡ <1 ms cycle
  • 🌐 Ethernet (RJ45)
  • 🚀 Real-time
  • 🎯 Motion-grade
Real Industrial Example
🚗

In an automotive body shop, Profinet synchronizes 40+ welding robots and conveyors with sub-millisecond precision — impossible with serial protocols like Modbus.

Quick Check

Q: Why can Profinet handle motion control but Modbus RTU cannot?

Tap to reveal answer ↓ A: Profinet uses real-time Ethernet with deterministic cycles under 1 ms, while Modbus RTU polls slaves one by one over slow serial lines — too slow and unpredictable for servo coordination.
