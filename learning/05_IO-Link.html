🔗IO-Link

The smart-sensor standard — Industry 4.0 starts here.

Quick Definition

IO-Link is a point-to-point digital protocol that connects smart sensors and actuators to a master device, carrying both process data and diagnostics over a standard 3-wire cable.

How It Connects
🖥️
PLC
Profinet / EtherNet/IP
📡
IO-Link Master
8 ports
🎯
Smart Sensor
3-wire M12

Bi-directional digital signal — data flows both ways

What IO-Link Carries
📊
Process Data

Live readings: distance, pressure, temperature, position

⚙️
Parameters

Configure sensor remotely — switching points, filters, ranges

🩺
Diagnostics

Health: temperature, operating hours, error codes

🆔
Identification

Vendor, model, serial — auto-detect on replacement

Key Facts
  • ✓ Point-to-point — one sensor per master port
  • ✓ Up to 20 m on standard 3-wire unshielded cable
  • ✓ Auto-parameter download on sensor swap (no recalibration)
  • ✓ Fieldbus-independent — works under Profinet, EtherNet/IP, EtherCAT
  • ✓ Foundation for predictive maintenance & Industry 4.0
Real Industrial Example
📦

In a packaging line, IO-Link photo-sensors report not just "object detected" but also signal strength, ambient light, and lens contamination — letting maintenance clean the sensor before it fails.
